Three ways of looking, one picture

No single moment tells the whole story, so the check-in combines three kinds of signals.

💬

What you’ve seen

Eight short questions about pointing, name response, pretend play and sounds.

👆

How they play

A gentle tap game where your child chooses between faces and shapes.

👋

How they respond

A timed name-call moment that notes how quickly they turn to you.

Your check-in

Takes about ten minutes. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Good to know

Is this a diagnosis?

No. Little Steps is a screening aid. Only a qualified clinician can evaluate a child for autism.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your answers stay in this browser tab and disappear when you close it.

What if the summary says everything looks typical?

If you still have concerns about your child’s development, speak to your pediatrician anyway. You know your child best.

How does the scoring work?

It uses simple, transparent rules that count flagged behaviors across the three parts. It is not a trained diagnostic model.
